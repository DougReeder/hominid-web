# Privacy Policy for Notes Together

The data you enter in Notes Together is accessible to anyone who uses the same account as you on a computer, and probably to the administrator.

If you designate a third-party server using the remoteStorage widget, your data will also be stored there.
Check with your provider for their privacy policy.

*Notes Together* does not collect nor transmit user data to its own server.
Nor does it collect or transmit data to third-parties that you haven't designated.
